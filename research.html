5D Supersymmetric Gauge Theories at Infinite Coupling: Brane Webs, Quivers, and Chiral Rings

PDF

September 2026

MSc dissertation · Imperial College London · Supervisor: Prof. Amihay Hanany

Investigated strongly coupled five-dimensional supersymmetric gauge theories using Type IIB brane webs, magnetic quivers, and algebraic methods. Derived explicit Higgs branch chiral ring relations for six SU(3) theories at infinite coupling. Recovered the finite-coupling glueball nilpotency across Chern-Simons levels, with evidence that successive glueball powers are supported on nested mesonic rank strata associated with nilpotent orbit closures.

Quantum Machine Learning

GitHub

Summer 2025

EPSRC-funded research · QET Labs, University of Bristol · With Dr Giulia Rubino, Dr Marius Krumm and Shivani Datye

Developed a differentiable JAX framework for simulating multi-photon learning in linear-optical networks. The architecture combines parameterised interferometers with repeated data encoding, with output statistics determined by matrix permanents arising from bosonic symmetrisation. Explored how photon loss, distinguishability and circuit parameterisation affect learning across different input Fock states, implementing gradient-based optimisation and scaling computational experiments to the BluePebble HPC cluster.

Lattice spin systems

PDF

Spring 2025

Extended lab project · University of Bristol · Supervisor: Prof. Francesco Turci

Evaluated the effectiveness of Metropolis and Wolff Monte Carlo algorithms in reproducing the established critical behaviour of the two-dimensional Ising model, using Binder cumulants and finite-size scaling to assess their performance and limitations. The comparison highlighted Wolff’s efficiency in reducing critical slowing down. Smaller investigations also explored the XY and Heisenberg models.
