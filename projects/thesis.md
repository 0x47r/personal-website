## Abstract

This dissertation gives a pedagogical account of a sequence of methods for probing strongly coupled five-dimensional *N* = 1 gauge theories of varying Chern–Simons level *k*, told through the calculations of SU(3)<sub>k</sub> + 5F for *k* = ½, ³⁄₂, ⁵⁄₂, and SU(3)<sub>k</sub> + 6F for *k* = 0, 1, 2. Beginning with field theory and moduli spaces, the discussion proceeds through Type IIB five-brane webs and magnetic quivers to Hilbert series, highest-weight generating functions and plethystic methods, culminating in an operator-level construction of the infinite–coupling Higgs-branch chiral ring relations for all six theories. These results extend the known highest-weight generating function counting data with explicit polynomial relations between the identified mesonic, baryonic, glueball and instanton sectors under the enhanced ultraviolet symmetry. The relations allow access to the non-reduced finite-coupling information invisible to the reduced Higgs variety; in particular, the nilpotency of the glueball is recovered in all six theories and is independent of the Chern–Simons level, while the relations further suggest that successive glueball powers are supported on increasingly constrained mesonic loci.
