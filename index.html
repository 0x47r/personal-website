I am a student of mathematical physics and science broadly as a discipline of mind. So far on my journey, I have been struck by the guiding principle of symmetry in quantum field theory. I have greatly enjoyed applying the methods of representation theory and algebraic geometry to the study of strongly coupled supersymmetric gauge theories. I am also drawn to the potential of modern computational tools, particularly machine learning. I believe that theoretical physics and learning systems can benefit from each other, and I am optimistic in how this relationship can influence a new era of scientific discovery.
